# Mock fashion photographs

These five local PNG files were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-14 for the Phase B UI prototype. They are fictional product photography, not photos of real inventory. No live Sanity image requests occur in the browser. Earlier copies of public catalog photos were discarded because their subjects did not match the approved reference style.

Final workspace assets: `dress.png`, `blazer.png`, `linen.png`, `pants.png`, `satin.png`, all in `apps/admin/public/demo/`.

## Final prompts

`dress.png`:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: single portrait product photograph for a minimal Hebrew boutique catalog UI prototype. A full-length realistic adult female fashion model wearing a flowing black satin maxi dress with fine spaghetti straps and a draped neckline, understated black sandals, hair in a sleek low bun. Standing naturally in front of warm pale beige limestone studio walls and ivory stone floor, softly lit daylight, muted neutral monochrome palette, refined European fashion editorial catalog photography. Front three-quarter view, full head-to-toe portrait, 3:4 aspect ratio, garment completely visible, model centered, ample clean margins. No text, no typography, no logos, no collage, no UI, no watermark. The dress is the primary subject.
